mc.w42.eu

A Minecraft server for family and friends, safe for very young players: whitelist only, and every player has a home cube of their own (512 × 512 blocks) where only they build and break things; in the other players' cubes they walk around and look, but cannot break, build or take anything.

A Paper server with mods of our own for the cube worlds. Every night the home cubes move around the grid, so there are new neighbours in the morning: a world rotation by our own Go tools on the go-mc26 library, with a backup before and after. Java Edition at cubes.mc.w42.eu, Bedrock (phones, tablets, consoles) at bedrock.mc.w42.eu.
