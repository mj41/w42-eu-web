s.w42.eu

M5Stack Stackchan robots in Embody Mode: the robot is a light client of servers and apps you choose, and you approve each one. Proofs of concept, vibe coded, not reviewed by humans yet.

The robot rendered in 3D from M5Stack's StackChan structure files, with real screens from it.

sm.w42.eustart here

The Stackchan manager: sign in with GitHub or Google, pick the apps for your robot and set it up with one click over USB (Chrome or Edge): the official firmware, your Wi-Fi and a token of its own for every app you approve.

Then it keeps a live line to your robots: switch their app, restart them, add or remove apps, see who is paired. One sign-in works for every app.

Apps

Each app has a host of its own under sa.w42.eu, so no app can read another's data in your browser. On the robot, swipe up for the app switcher; on a phone, scan the robot's QR code to pair.

Raw data

Every sensor and command: see through its camera, hear it, talk through it, move its head, set its face and LEDs, read IR remotes and NFC tags. With end to end encryption on, only paired browsers can read the robot: the server relays ciphertext.

Pet

A Tamagotchi for kids, on the robot and on a phone: feed it, pet it, play with it, put it to bed, or hold an NFC card to it to feed it. It shows how it feels, and never suffers while the kid sleeps or is away.

Focus

A focus timer for your desk: 25 minutes of focus, then a break. Tap the robot to start or pause; it chimes, nods and dims its LEDs as time runs out. Tasks, a daily goal and the week's count on your phone.

How it works

The robot connects out to one app at a time over a WebSocket; nothing connects in to it. Each app gets a token for just that robot, from the manager or one you make yourself. The firmware is built reproducibly: anyone can rebuild a release and get the same bytes, and each release is signed.
