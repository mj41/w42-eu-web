s.w42.eu

M5Stack Stackchan robots in Embody Mode: the robot is a light client of servers and apps you choose, and you approve each one. Proofs of concept, vibe coded, not reviewed by humans yet.

sm.w42.eustart here

The Stackchan manager: sign in with GitHub or Google, pick the apps for your robot and set it up with one click over USB (Chrome or Edge): the official firmware and a token of its own for every app you approve.

Other ways to set up a robot, e.g. with servers of your own at home.

Apps

Each app has a host of its own under sa.w42.eu, so no app can read another's data in your browser.

  • raw.sa.w42.eu: every sensor and command. Pair a phone by the QR code on the robot's screen, then see through its camera, hear it, talk through it and move it.
  • pet.sa.w42.eu: a Tamagotchi for kids, on the robot and on a phone. soon
  • dev.sa.w42.eu: a higher level API for developers and AI agents. planned
