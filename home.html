home.w42.eu

A local first, security and privacy first platform for a home. This page is only a pointer: the up to date information lives in the GitHub repositories below.

The ideadesign and proofs of concept

Go servers on a small machine at home, every device (new or old) a light client of them, and loops that AI helps you write and you approve. Restricted by default, data stays at home. The first devices are M5Stack Stackchan robots, a pet app for kids and a micro:bit car.

All proofs of concept, vibe coded, not reviewed by humans yet.

Design

  • home-w42-eu: vision, use cases (the main driver), principles, architecture and the device wire protocol. Apache-2.0
  • home-w42-eu-ideas: devices, adapters and loops, each from who needs it: old phones, a Roomba, Home Assistant, cameras, calendars, local voice. Apache-2.0

Stackchan robots

  • StackChan firmware, Embody Mode: the robot as a light client of a server you choose, switchable between servers and apps. Set up a robot. MIT
  • s-w42-eu-manager: sign-in, your robots, the apps you approve, one-click setup over USB. Runs at sm.w42.eu. MIT
  • s-w42-eu-raw: relay and dashboard, pair a phone by the QR code on the robot's screen. Runs at raw.sa.w42.eu. MIT
  • s-w42-eu-pet: a Tamagotchi for kids, on the robot and on a phone. MIT

Home node and a car

  • s-w42-eu-sbot: the seed of the home node, a web/API server with a cockpit, an event hub and a controller server for loops. Apache-2.0
  • tpbot-ble: micro:bit V2 firmware for the ELECFREAKS TPBot car, a BLE peripheral with raw sensors. Apache-2.0
